## ✨ What Was Accomplished

---

## 🎯 Feature Summary

### The Problem Solved
Previously, the device ran at the same vibration frequency 24/7, which meant:
- ❌ Effective deterrence during day but potentially noisy at night
- ❌ No way to adapt to different time periods
- ❌ Wasted power running full intensity during sleeping hours

### The Solution
Now the device automatically switches between two independent configurations:

**🌞 Day Settings (8 AM - 8 PM)**
- More frequent vibrations for active mole deterrence
- Default: 1 vibration every 30 seconds
- Optimized for maximum effectiveness when moles are most active

**🌙 Night Settings (8 PM - 8 AM)**
- Much less frequent vibrations to respect neighbors
- Default: 1 vibration every 90 seconds (1/3 frequency)
- Saves ~70% power during night hours
- Still provides deterrence but minimally disruptive

### How It Works
1. Device queries current time via NTP (Network Time Protocol)
2. Compares current hour against night boundaries (8 PM - 8 AM)
3. Automatically selects appropriate settings (day or night)
4. Motor operates with selected timing parameters
5. Switching happens transparently without manual intervention

### Default Configuration

| Setting | Day | Night | Ratio |
|---------|-----|-------|-------|
| Run Time | 2-6s | 2-4s | 67% |
| Rest Time | 20-40s | 60-120s | 3x longer |
| Motor Speed | 200-255 PWM | 200-255 PWM | Same |
| Frequency | ~1/30s | ~1/90s | 1/3 |

## 🔧 How to Use

### For End Users
